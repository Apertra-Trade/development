Common questions

Why is my shipment on hold?

Almost always a document: a label, a certificate or a prior notice. The shipment screen names the exact item, and freight releases automatically once it is uploaded.

How does escrow release work?

Funds are held by our regulated payments partner and release when the shipment clears customs and is delivered. If a supplier never ships, the full amount returns automatically.

What does Apertra charge?

A 1.25% transaction fee embedded in each trade, plus your membership, which is credited against that month's fees. Buyers always join free.

Who is liable if an HS code is wrong?

Classifications are prepared with their reasoning and reviewed by a licensed broker before filing, and the full audit trail is kept for seven years.

Can I cancel a buying request?

Yes, any time before the supplier ships. Once shipped, the escrow terms on the request apply.

Pay into escrow

Your money is held by Apertra's regulated payments partner, not by the supplier and not by Apertra. It releases only when the goods clear customs and are delivered to you.

Release conditions: supplier ships with Apertra documents, customs clears at destination, you confirm delivery (or 48 hours pass after delivery scan). If the supplier never ships, the full amount returns to you automatically.
