Apertra · Open every market

Sell across borders without hiring a trade department.

Apertra is a full mesh network for cross-border commerce, not a Canada-centric hub. A business based in Canada, the US, Europe, Australia or New Zealand can sign up, originate a shipment to any of the other four regions, and import from any of them, on the same rails: classification, true landed cost before you quote, customs-ready documents, freight, and protected payment in one workflow. An exporter in Australia selling to the US, a winery in New Zealand selling to Europe, a brand in Europe selling to Canada: same account, same record, same protection.
